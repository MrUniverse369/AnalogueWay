# Harvey's Nine Lives: session notes (17 October 2026)

This session covered three rounds of work:

1. A zone-select overworld map.
2. A restyle of that map in the HNL art style.
3. A large expansion: main menu redesign, six levels per zone, three new bosses, and an animation clean-up.

Everything in rounds 2 and 3 is **uncommitted**. Your last commit is `4b50c73 zone select map`.

---

## Round 1: the zone-select map

**Request:** a flat overworld stage select, inspired by 90s/2000s games (SMB3, Shovel Knight, Hyper Light Drifter references), reachable from a new **Zone Select** button on the main menu.

**What was built**

- **Scene:** `Assets/Scenes/ZoneSelect.unity`, added at the end of Build Settings so no existing build index moved.
- **Scripts:**
  - `ZoneSelectMap.cs` runs the map: Harvey walks node to node along the paths using his real idle and run frames. Cross/Enter plays the level and Circle/Esc returns to the menu. You can't walk onto locked nodes; Harvey shakes instead.
  - `ZoneMapNode.cs` holds each node's data.
- **Unlocking:**
  - Levels open up to the furthest level reached. This is stored as `SaveData.furthestScene` and survives starting a New Game.
  - Starting a level from the map keeps your saved coins and gives full lives.
- **Main menu:** a Zone Select button calling the new `MenueControl.LoadZoneSelect()`, with Play → Continue → Zone Select navigation that wraps round.
- **Art:** generated by `Tools/Art/zone_map.py` (pure Python, no Pillow).
- **Font:** DOS VGA pixel font (`Assets/Fonts/PerfectDOSVGA437` plus a TMP SDF asset).

## Round 2: restyle in the HNL art style

**Feedback:** the first version looked too much like Shovel Knight, especially the black banner. The map's hard edges should be smoothed, and the art should follow the HNL style in `/Applications/PixelArt/Treasured Traverse`.

**Changes**

- **Black banner:** removed completely. A small label in the HNL HUD-frame style (cream, yellow and orange rim, purple inside) now floats above the selected node. The coin counter and the controls hint use matching plates.
- **Smoother shapes:**
  - The map is now a **rounded island** in the sea.
  - The **path bends are curved**.
  - The **zone borders are soft curves**.
- **Your palettes:**
  - TT stone ramp.
  - Egypt sand waves.
  - Japan navy and snow.
  - Teal grass with a lime lip over plum dirt.
  - Clumpy three-tone trees like TreeOneFinal.
- **Level markers:** talisman-style gems on stone pads. The goal uses your treasure pedestal.
- **Small touches:** drifting clouds and a pulsing selection ring.
- **Layout file:** the map script writes `Tools/Art/zone_map_layout.txt` (node and path positions), so the walkable route always matches the art.

## Round 3: the big expansion

### 1. Main menu redesign

- **Scene:** a warm dawn over an archaeological dig.
  - Harvey stands beside a **half-buried stone cat idol** he has just uncovered.
  - The dig has crates, a pickaxe, a rope grid, a ladder pit and a lantern.
  - A sandy road winds toward the sunrise, and the horizon shows silhouettes of all four zones (temple, pyramids, snowy pagoda mountain, green hills).
- **Title:** chunky pixel lettering "HARVEY'S NINE LIVES" in the HUD colours.
- **Buttons:** HUD-frame plates with icons: gem for Play, journal for Continue, compass for Zone Select.
- **Selection:** a bobbing **paw pointer** marks the selected button.
- **Animated details:** Harvey idles, the lantern flickers, dust drifts and the title bobs (`Assets/Scripts/MainMenuScene.cs`).
- **Music:** **"Harvey's Theme"** (`Assets/Music/HarveysTheme.wav`, from `Tools/Music/main_theme.py`). It runs about 74 s in a seamless loop at 104 BPM in D major, moving through a flute intro, a brass theme, a bell bridge and a theme reprise.
- **Kept as they were:** the existing buttons, their OnClick bindings and the navigation. The old brick frame, panel and text title are switched off, not deleted.

New scenes are in **bold**. Win and ZoneSelect follow. Z4A1–A4 and the old numbered levels stay disabled.

**How the drafts are made:**

- **Tools:** `Assets/Editor/DraftLevelBuilder.cs` and `ZoneContentBuilder.cs`. The menu item **Tools → HNL → Rebuild Draft Levels and Boss Arenas** asks first, because it overwrites those scenes.
- **Starting point:** each draft copies a real level of its zone (Tomb from Z1A1, Sandy from Z2A2, Japan from Z3A1, Meadow from Z4A6), keeping the player, UI, music, background and enemy types.
- **Layout:** the terrain is cleared and a new layout is generated with flat runs, gaps, staircases, floating platforms and pillar hops, with difficulty rising through each zone.
- **Tiles:** the zone's own tiles, chosen automatically from how the original level uses them.
- **Jumps:** gaps are limited to what Harvey can clear (jump apex about 1.16 tiles; gaps of 2, or 3 when dropping down).
- **Contents:** coins, patrolling enemies, a midpoint checkpoint and an end treasure.
- **Arenas:** each boss arena is a walled floor in the zone's tiles, with the boss rig copied from Z3BossBattle.

**Zone select:** 25 nodes. Boss nodes are horned skulls placed beside their zone's landmark. The scene is rebuilt by `Assets/Editor/ZoneSelectSceneBuilder.cs` (**Tools → HNL → Rebuild Zone Select Scene**).

### 3. Bosses

All four use the Japan boss's rule: **3 lives, 3 stages, hittable only while slumped in the opening at the end of each pattern**. New move types were appended to `YokaiBoss.cs` (marked `BOSSES:`), so no code was duplicated and the Japan boss's saved patterns are unchanged.

- **Teleport:** fades out, reappears above the player, then drops (with a slam in later stages).
- **Burrow:** sinks into a low, jumpable mound that hunts the player, then erupts.
- **Volley:** fires a row of shots that alternate low (jump them) and high (stay down).
- **GroundSpikes:** thorns warn at the floor, then burst upward; the first one targets the player.

| Boss | Stage 1 | Stage 2 | Stage 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Grave Warden** (stone golem, green spirit flames) | Volley, Charge | Teleport, Volley | Teleport, RockRain, Volley |
| **Sandscale Scarab** (gold scarab, lapis gem) | Burrow | Burrow (shockwaves), Charge | Burrow, Volley, RockRain |
| **King Mossback** (crowned mossy toad) | LeapSlam ×2 | GroundSpikes, LeapSlam | GroundSpikes, Volley, LeapSlam |
| Yokai Warrior (Japan) | unchanged | unchanged | unchanged |

Boss and hazard art comes from `Tools/Art/bosses.py`, saved to `Assets/Sprites/Enemies/Bosses/`.

### 4. Animation review

- **Samurai monkey:** a new **SwordDraw** state plays after every three walk loops: grip, draw, raise, slash with an arc, follow-through, sheathe, then a glint. He keeps walking throughout, and his collider and combat are unchanged.
  - Frames: `JSEAnim_Sword.png` from `Tools/Art/monkey_sword.py`, 49×46, centre pivots so the body lines up with the walk frames.
- **Player:**
  - The jump no longer restarts every frame.
  - All pose switches are instant (sprites can't blend, so the old 0.1–0.25 s durations just made poses linger).
  - Damage and StandingDamage now actually return to Movement. Before, Unity ignored those exits.
- **Enemies:** death animations play once in full instead of re-entering before their last frame.
- **Chest and tori gate:** instant transitions.

## Fixes made along the way

- **Generated floors had no collision:** the Tomb fill tile has a tiny physics shape. The generator now paints grid-collider copies of the zone tiles (`Assets/Sprites/Tileset/GridColliderTiles/`); the originals are untouched.
- **Boss rigs rendered black in Sandy:** the 2D global light didn't reach the rig's sorting layer. The rig now uses unlit Sprites-Default.
- **Zone select locked everything:** the save's furthest level (`Z4A4GrassGreen`) is a disabled scene. `GameSession.FurthestBuildIndex` now falls back to the save's current level.
