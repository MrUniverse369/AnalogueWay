// ------------------------------------------------------------------ Farmer Clover, by the barn
== clover
-> cycle: clover_first, clover_again

== clover_first
Clover: Well now, a traveller! You've come a long way by the look of those paws.
* Where am I? -> clover_place
* Any advice? -> clover_tip
* Just passing. -> clover_bye

== clover_place
Clover: The last village before home, they say. Green hills as far as you like.
Mind the moss king in the woods. He doesn't care for trespassers.
-> clover_tip

== clover_tip
Clover: The bouncy clouds will carry you over the worst of it.
And keep some lobster handy. Nothing mends a cat like lobster.
-> clover_bye

== clover_again
Clover: Back again? The cows have missed you. Well, one of them has.
-> end

== clover_bye
Clover: Safe travels, and wipe your feet on the way out.
-> end


// ------------------------------------------------------------------ Old Willow, on the bench
== willow
Willow: Nine lives, have you? I had nine once. Now I have a very comfy bench.
* What happened to the others? -> willow_lives
* Enjoy the bench. -> willow_bye

== willow_lives
Willow: Spent them all on adventures, every one worth it.
Use yours well, young one. Home is just over the hill.
-> willow_bye

== willow_bye
Willow: Off you go. I'll be right here.
-> end
