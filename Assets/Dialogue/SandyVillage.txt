// ------------------------------------------------------------------ Amun, the bazaar merchant
== amun
-> cycle: amun_first, amun_again

== amun_first
Amun: Welcome to the oasis, friend! Water, shade and the finest fish this side of the dunes.
* What is this place? -> amun_place
* Any advice for the desert? -> amun_tip
* Goodbye. -> amun_bye

== amun_place
Amun: Every caravan stops here. The palms keep the sand off, mostly.
Past the dunes lies the Scarab's nest. Nobody stops there.
-> amun_tip

== amun_tip
Amun: Sharks swim in the sand. Watch for the fins, then jump.
And keep a fish on your quick button: R1 in a pinch is worth two in the bag!
-> amun_bye

== amun_again
Amun: The friend with whiskers returns! Business is good when you're about.
-> end

== amun_bye
Amun: May the wind be at your tail.
-> end


// ------------------------------------------------------------------ Nefi, by the well
== nefi
Nefi: Shh. If you listen at the well you can hear the old river underneath.
* What river? -> nefi_river
* I'll leave you to it. -> nefi_bye

== nefi_river
Nefi: The one that made the pyramids rich. It sank into the sand long ago.
My grandmother says the Scarab drank it all.
-> nefi_bye

== nefi_bye
Nefi: Stay cool out there.
-> end
